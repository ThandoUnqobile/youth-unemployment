The issue

South Africa’s youth unemployment rate was 47.4% for people aged 15–34 in Q2 2026, according to Statistics South Africa. Finding trustworthy opportunities and a clear first step can be difficult.

Source: Stats SA, Quarterly Labour Force Survey Q2 2026. The figure may change in future quarters.
